# Health Logging Guide

## Overview

HealthLog AI provides comprehensive health and wellness tracking through intuitive manual logging. Our system combines precise data entry with AI-powered insights to help you achieve your health goals.

## Features

### 🏃‍♂️ Fitness Tracking

Track your physical activities with detailed logging:

- **Activity Types**: Running, weightlifting, yoga, cycling, swimming, etc.
- **Duration & Intensity**: Log time spent and effort level (low, medium, high)
- **Calories & Distance**: Monitor energy expenditure and distance covered
- **Weight Training**: Track sets, reps, and weights used
- **Environmental Factors**: Location, weather conditions
- **Notes**: Personal observations and feelings

### 🍎 Nutrition Logging

Comprehensive meal and nutrition tracking:

- **Meal Types**: Breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks
- **Food Items**: JSON array of detailed food breakdowns
- **Macronutrients**: Protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, sugar, sodium
- **Calorie Counting**: Total energy intake monitoring
- **Meal Notes**: Preparation methods, satisfaction levels
- **Mood Tracking**: Before and after meal mood assessment

### 😊 Mood & Wellness

Monitor your mental and emotional wellbeing:

- **Mood Rating**: 1-10 scale for daily mood assessment
- **Energy Levels**: Track energy throughout the day (1-10 scale)
- **Stress Levels**: Monitor stress with 1-10 scale
- **Sleep Quality**: Rate sleep quality and track hours
- **Water Intake**: Track daily water consumption
- **Steps Count**: Monitor daily step count
- **Weight Tracking**: Log daily weight measurements
- **Activities**: JSON array of activities that affect your mood
- **Wellness Notes**: Personal reflections and observations

### 🏋️‍♂️ Routine Management

Create and follow structured workout and nutrition routines:

- **Simple Routines**: Pre-built workout templates
- **Custom Routines**: Create personalized workout plans
- **Nutrition Routines**: Structured meal planning
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor routine adherence
- **Workout Logging**: Track routine completion
- **Meal Logging**: Track nutrition routine progress

## Best Practices

### Consistent Logging
- Log activities immediately after completion
- Use routine templates for consistent workout tracking
- Set daily reminders for mood and energy tracking
- Log meals as you eat them for accuracy
- Review and update entries regularly

### Accurate Data Entry
- Be specific with portion sizes and measurements
- Include all ingredients in meals as JSON arrays
- Note environmental factors that might affect performance
- Record honest mood and energy assessments
- Use consistent units (kg, minutes, ml) for measurements

### Goal Setting
- Set realistic, measurable health goals
- Use routine templates to structure your approach
- Break large goals into smaller milestones
- Regularly review and adjust objectives
- Celebrate progress and achievements

### Routine Management
- Start with simple routines before creating custom ones
- Follow routine schedules consistently
- Log workout and meal completions
- Adjust routines based on progress
- Use AI insights to optimize your approach
