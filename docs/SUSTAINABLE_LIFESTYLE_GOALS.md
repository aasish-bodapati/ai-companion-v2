# Sustainable Lifestyle Goals Philosophy

## 🌱 Vision: Balanced Discipline for Long-term Health

Our health and wellness app is built on the principle of **sustainable lifestyle goals** rather than extreme, time-bound targets. We focus on building **balanced discipline** that people can maintain indefinitely without hating the process.

## 🎯 Core Philosophy

### The Problem with Traditional Goal Setting
- **Extreme time pressure**: "Lose 20kg in 3 months"
- **Crash diet mentality**: Unsustainable restrictions
- **Workout burnout**: Excessive frequency leading to injury
- **All-or-nothing thinking**: Perfectionism that leads to failure
- **Vanity metrics**: Focus on appearance over health

### Our Solution: Sustainable Lifestyle Anchors
- **No time pressure**: Goals to work toward indefinitely
- **Realistic targets**: Achievable with consistent, moderate effort
- **Health-focused**: Based on actual health markers
- **Flexible progress**: Success at any stage of the journey
- **Habit building**: Focus on sustainable behaviors

## 📊 Current Goal Framework

### Body Composition Targets (Lifestyle Anchors)

#### Target BMI: 24.0
- **Why**: Healthy, maintainable range for most people
- **Approach**: Not underweight, not overweight, just right
- **Sustainability**: Achievable with consistent habits
- **Flexibility**: People can be anywhere on the spectrum and still benefit

#### Target Body Fat: 15%
- **Why**: Healthy range for most individuals
- **Approach**: Not extreme lean, not high body fat
- **Sustainability**: Maintainable long-term
- **Flexibility**: Focus on direction, not perfection

#### Waist-to-Height Ratio: 0.47
- **Why**: Strong health marker for metabolic health
- **Approach**: Long-term health indicator
- **Sustainability**: Can be maintained with lifestyle habits
- **Flexibility**: Gradual improvement over time

#### Target FFMI: 22
- **Why**: Achievable muscle mass for most people
- **Approach**: Realistic strength and muscle goals
- **Sustainability**: Maintainable with regular activity
- **Flexibility**: Personalized based on individual body composition

### Nutritional Guidelines (Daily Habits)

#### Calorie Targets: Maintenance Level
- **Why**: No extreme cutting or bulking cycles
- **Approach**: Sustainable energy balance
- **Sustainability**: Can be maintained indefinitely
- **Flexibility**: Personalized based on individual needs

#### Protein Targets: Comprehensive 6-Case Formula
- **Why**: Personalized based on actual body composition
- **Approach**: Scientific calculation, not arbitrary rules
- **Sustainability**: Practical and achievable
- **Flexibility**: Adapts to available data (FFM, SMM, Body Fat %)

## 🌟 Key Principles

### 1. No Time Pressure
- Goals are **lifestyle anchors**, not temporary targets
- People can work toward these **indefinitely**
- No crash course mentality
- Focus on **being** healthy rather than **getting** healthy

### 2. Realistic Expectations
- Targets are **achievable** for most people
- Based on **health markers**, not vanity metrics
- **Gradual progress** without self-punishment
- **Consistent effort** over extreme measures

### 3. Flexible Progress
- People can be **"far from"** goals and still feel good about progress
- No shame in being **"near"** the target
- **Direction over perfection**
- **Process over outcome**

### 4. Habit Building
- Focus on **building sustainable habits**
- **Lifestyle integration** rather than temporary changes
- **Gentle, consistent pressure** toward health
- **Enjoy the process** rather than hate it
