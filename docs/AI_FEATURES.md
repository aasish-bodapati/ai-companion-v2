# AI Features Guide

## Overview

HealthLog AI leverages advanced artificial intelligence to provide personalized health coaching, pattern recognition, and actionable insights based on your logged data.

## Core AI Capabilities

### 🧠 Pattern Recognition

Our AI analyzes your health data to identify meaningful patterns:

- **Diet-Energy Correlations**: Discover which foods boost or drain your energy
- **Exercise-Mood Links**: Find the optimal workout timing for your mood
- **Sleep-Performance Patterns**: Identify how sleep affects your daily performance
- **Stress Triggers**: Recognize activities or situations that impact your wellbeing
- **Seasonal Trends**: Track how weather and seasons affect your health

### 🎯 Personalized Recommendations

Get tailored advice based on your unique data:

- **Workout Suggestions**: AI-recommended exercises based on your goals and progress
- **Nutrition Guidance**: Personalized meal suggestions and dietary adjustments
- **Mood Optimization**: Strategies to improve energy and emotional wellbeing
- **Goal Adjustment**: Dynamic goal setting based on your actual progress
- **Habit Formation**: AI-powered strategies to build healthy habits

### 📊 Predictive Analytics

Anticipate trends and optimize your health journey:

- **Progress Forecasting**: Predict your trajectory toward health goals
- **Risk Identification**: Early warning signs for potential health issues
- **Optimal Timing**: Best times for workouts, meals, and rest
- **Plateau Detection**: Identify when you might hit progress plateaus
- **Success Probability**: Likelihood of achieving specific goals
