# Companion App Concept

## Onboarding and Daily Routine
After installation, I answer a few basic questions during onboarding and then land on the **Companion Page**. Here, I can start small talk with the companion and share my daily routines, habits, and goals.  

For example, my current routine looks like this:

- **4:30 AM** – Wake up  
- **5:00–6:30 AM** – Workout (Monday to Saturday)  
- **7:00 AM** – Back home  
- **8:00 AM** – Breakfast  
  - 4 boiled eggs  
  - Salad with assorted greens  
  - Protein shake (27g protein powder, 250ml milk, 1 banana, 2 tbsp peanut butter)  
  - Supplements: D3+K2 and fish oil capsules  
- **9:30 AM** – Leave for work  
- **10:30 AM** – Reach office  
- **12:00 PM** – Snack: 1 carrot  
- **2:00 PM** – Lunch  
  - 2 cups of white rice  
  - Curry (usually chicken or fish + greens cooked by my mom)  
- **4:00 PM** – Fruit salad (ordered from outside)  
- **6:30 PM** – Leave work  
- **7:30 PM** – Reach home  
- **8:00 PM** – Dinner  
  - 250g air-fried mixed chicken  
  - Salad with assorted greens  
- **8:30 PM onwards** – Work on my app, take a short walk  
- **9:30 PM** – Bedtime  

These recurring activities could be automatically added to a **calendar** or **Today Dashboard** with notifications and checkboxes to mark progress.

---

## Workout Tracking
I can either:
1. **Discuss my workout plan** with the companion, confirm details, and let it track my routine,  
or  
2. **Directly share my workout plan** if I already have one.  

During the first week, I can log the weights and reps for each exercise. In the following weeks:  
- If I do the same routine, I can simply say *“same as last week.”*  
- If I change something, I can say *“I increased squats by 2.5kg”* or *“I did lighter bench press this week.”*  

The companion remembers and updates my workout log accordingly.

---

## Nutrition Tracking
Since I’ve already shared my daily eating habits, I can ask the companion to help me meet nutritional goals.  
For example:  
- **Goal:** 2500 calories, 150g protein, and sufficient fiber per day.  

If my day doesn’t align with the usual schedule (e.g., skipped a meal, ate out, or added extra snacks), I can simply tell the companion. It will update my nutrition log and keep tracking against my goals.

---

## Dashboards
The app should provide:  
- **Fitness Dashboard** – Track workouts, progressions, weights, and consistency.  
- **Nutrition Dashboard** – Track calories, macros (protein, carbs, fat), and micronutrient/fiber goals.  

---

## Vision
The app acts as a **bridge between current lifestyle and desired lifestyle**, giving **insights, reminders, and motivation** to help me live better.
